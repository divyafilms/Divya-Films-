Meet Aditya Kumar — This Is All Rounder In Photo

Photographer • Cinematographer • Video Editor • Drone Pilot

Aditya Kumar is the founder, owner, and creative backbone of 'Divya Films.' Unlike traditional studio owners who merely outsource tasks or manage schedules, Aditya is a highly skilled 'all-rounder' who remains personally involved in every aspect of the work. From capturing portraits of royal brides and operating 5.1K aerial drones to maneuvering a 3-axis cinema gimbal on the dance floor and editing 4K cinematic films in the editing suite—Aditya handles every stage of visual storytelling with immense passion and perfection.

Furthermore, Aditya Kumar is academy-certified and available for assignments anywhere across India.

With over 10 years of hands-on experience and having covered more than 550+ royal weddings and numerous engagement shoots—across locations including Mirganj, Gopalganj, Siwan, barhriya, Patna, Gorakhpur, Chhapra, Varanasi, and various luxury destinations throughout India—Aditya seamlessly blends cinematic techniques with a deep respect for the cultural wedding traditions of Bihar and the Purvanchal region.

Lead Photographer

As the lead photographer, he specializes in capturing candid emotions, portraits of royal brides, the sacred rituals at the mandap, and creating memorable family photography that lasts a lifetime.

Master Cinematography

Featuring 5.1K aerial cinematic drone coverage, shots captured with the Sony FX3, Sony M5, and other Cinema Line cameras, 3-axis Ronin gimbal footage, and slow-motion teasers.

Creative Videos Editing

Hollywood-style color grading, music-beat syncing, viral-ready Instagram Reels (delivered in 48 hours), curated wedding reels, and exceptional wedding films.

Unified Professionalism on Every Wedding Set

We maintain a consistent professional standard at every wedding or event. Our team arrives on-site wearing 'Divya Films' uniforms (polo shirts). They carry essential professional identification, wireless communication headsets, and backup camera equipment. We believe that treating the elders of the family with respect and being punctual are just as important as capturing high-quality 4K footage—and that maintaining a professional, courteous demeanor increases the likelihood of future work opportunities.

The Foundation

Our Four Pillars of Artistic Excellence

At Divya Films, we have four pillars of artistic excellence—principles that guide every frame we capture.

Emotional Authenticity

At this studio, we prioritize emotional authenticity—valuing genuine tears and laughter far more than staged poses. Our team captures real moments exactly as they unfold.

Cinema-Grade Aesthetics

With a cinematic aesthetic—utilizing 4K 10-bit color profiles and custom, Hollywood-style LUTs—our films evoke the look and feel of romantic movies meant for the big screen.

Family-Centric Warmth

Wherever we go, our team integrates seamlessly, working alongside everyone like family. We approach every task with sincerity and hold your traditions and customs in the highest regard.

Timely Delivery

We understand how eager you are to see your wedding photos and videos; that is why we ensure that your teasers and albums are delivered well within the promised timeframe.

Technical Precision

State-of-the-art Cinema Technology

We invest in some of the finest equipment available in the industry, ensuring that your memories shine through our work with crystal-clear 4K resolution.

DJI RS4 Gimbal Stabilizer

This 3-axis electronic gimbal, built with carbon fiber, ensures perfectly smooth and cinematic footage as the bride walks toward the mandap (wedding altar).

Godox and Aputure Studio Strobes

With a soft and warm lighting setup, these lights allow for the capture of the bride’s jewelry, lehenga (wedding attire), and skin tone beautifully—all without causing any glare or discomfort to the guests' eyes.

Sennheiser and Røde wireless audio

Broadcast-grade lapel microphones—perfect for capturing Sanskrit mantras (as chanted by a pandit ji). They record the audio with such clarity that, during the editing process, the original voice can be preserved perfectly, resulting in a crisp, high-quality sound.

Indian flush-mount lay-flat albums

These are produced using commercial-grade photographic printers. The pages are waterproof and fingerprint-resistant, and they are durable enough not to tear even when handled frequently. These albums are bound in genuine leather or velvet covers.
