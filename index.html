Meet Aditya Kumar — This Is All Rounder In Photo

The owner of the studio is an all-rounder.

Aditya Kumar is the founder, owner, and creative backbone of 'Divya Films.' Unlike traditional studio owners who merely outsource tasks or manage schedules, Aditya is a highly skilled 'all-rounder' who remains personally involved in every aspect of the work. From capturing portraits of royal brides and operating 5.1K aerial drones to maneuvering a 3-axis cinema gimbal on the dance floor and editing 4K cinematic films in the editing suite—Aditya handles every stage of visual storytelling with immense passion and perfection.

Furthermore, Aditya Kumar is academy-certified and available for assignments anywhere across India.

With over 10 years of hands-on experience and having covered more than 550+ royal weddings and numerous engagement shoots—across locations including Mirganj, Gopalganj, Siwan, barhriya, Patna, Gorakhpur, Chhapra, Varanasi, and various luxury destinations throughout India—Aditya seamlessly blends cinematic techniques with a deep respect for the cultural wedding traditions of Bihar and the Purvanchal region.

Everything You Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about booking, deliverables, or gear? Here are answers.
How far in advance should we reserve our wedding date with Divya Films?
We recommend booking your date at least 2 to 6 months in advance, especially during the auspicious wedding seasons. Because Aditya Kumar personally directs every wedding to ensure 100% focus and quality, dates fill up quickly.
Do you travel outside Gopalganj for weddings across Bihar and India?
Yes! While our head studio is on Rice Mil Road Badarjimi ( Mirganj Gopalganj ), our team regularly travels throughout Bihar (Patna, Siwan, Muzaffarpur, Gaya), Uttar Pradesh (Gorakhpur, Varanasi, Lucknow), and destination wedding locations across India.
When and how do we receive our final wedding photos and cinematic film?
You will receive your 48-Hour Instagram Reel teaser within 2 days of your wedding! Full color-graded high-resolution photos are delivered on our private online Client Gallery within 2 to 3 weeks, and the master 4K wedding film and flush-mount printed album within 4 to 5 weeks.
Can we customize our photography package?
Absolutely. You can customize between our 25k, 35k, 55k, 85k, 105k, and 155k packages or add extra drone days, pre-wedding films, additional photographer units, or LED walls using our interactive calculator on our Packages page or by discussing directly with Aditya Kumar on WhatsApp.
