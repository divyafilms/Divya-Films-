Why our flush-mount albums stand miles ahead of standard photo print books

180° Layflat Binding

No gutter loss! Open your album completely flat across both pages. Grand panoramic mandap and baraat photos display seamlessly across a huge 36-inch spread without any split or distortion.

Waterproof & Anti-Fingerprint

Every page is sealed with a microscopic thermal matte luster lamination. Spilled water, chai splashes, or oily fingerprints wipe off instantly with a soft cloth without harming the prints.

Heavy Rigid Core Pages

Pages do not bend or curl like ordinary paper. Each sheet is mounted on an archival 1.2mm composite rigid coreboard, giving each page turn a weighty, luxurious feel.
